Tesla Trip Brain

DEMO DATA — no real car connected

Tesla nav reacts en route. Trip Brain answers the night before: am I ready for tomorrow's trip? Pick a pretend trip, set your battery %, and see the same math the real app runs — drive energy + reserve + preconditioning.

How the math works (the real app's rules)

Base 280 Wh/mile, adjusted for destination weather: below 40°F +15% (below 20°F +25%), above 95°F +10%. Preconditioning: 2.0 kWh / 30 min on cold or hot days, else 0.5 kWh / 15 min. Reserve: 10% of a 75 kWh pack (7.5 kWh). Required departure SoC = (drive + preconditioning + reserve) ÷ 75 kWh.

This page is static hosting only — it never talks to a car or Tesla's servers. The full app is free & open source: github.com/rtmalikian/tesla-trip-brain.
